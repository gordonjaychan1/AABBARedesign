About the academy

High-level instruction. World-class athletes.

AABBA is well known for its high-level instruction and world-class athletes, but most notably for its holistic methods to teach skills with an emphasis on health and fitness wellness.

Shihan Hultin has had a positive impact on students’ lives while developing lasting relationships that will be remembered for years to come.

Respect

Bowing in, bowing out, and treating everyone on the floor well.

Discipline

Showing up, working hard and finishing what you start.

Humility

Every belt is the start of the next thing to learn.
